Umbrella math

One shoulder always gets wet, the "two-person" umbrella fits one, and a third umbrella a year vanishes into cafés and gusts. Umbrella math turns canopy centimeters into honest coverage and people sheltered, sideways rain into the dry margin you actually keep, and your loss rate into what the habit costs a year. Exact arithmetic, every borrowed number labeled.

Open the calculators

The three tools

  • Cover plan - canopy size becomes square meters and people sheltered.
  • Dry plan - canopy, shoulders and sideways drift become your real dry margin.
  • Year plan - umbrellas lost or broken become months each and cost per year.

Labeled norms

Labeled dome: a canopy covers about 85% of its full circle. Labeled shoulder space: 0.3 m² per person. Labeled drift: 15 cm of sideways rain in a breeze. Labeled habit: 1.5 umbrellas lost or broken a year. These are reference values, not rules - your canopy, your weather and your memory decide the real numbers.
